Why South Bay Pup Club is just me

Most dog-walking companies grow by adding walkers and dogs to every walk. Your dog ends up in a pack of six, with a different person at the door every few weeks.

I went the other way. South Bay Pup Club is just me, and I care for one household's dogs at a time. I learn your dog's quirks: the corner they're nervous about, the treat that works, how they like their belly rubbed. You get one person who knows your dog and answers your texts.

Because I do every visit myself, I take on a limited number of families. If we're a fit, your dog gets my whole attention.

— Tom

FAQ

Good questions

Will it really always be you?

Yes. South Bay Pup Club is me, with no staff and no subcontractors. If I'm ever sick or away, I'll tell you well in advance and won't send a stranger.

Do you walk dogs together?

No. Every walk is private: one household's dogs at a time. If you have two dogs, they walk together with me (+$15).

How does boarding work?

Your dog stays in my home and sleeps indoors, never in a kennel. I board one family at a time, so your dog won't share space with strange dogs. I send daily photos and updates.

Can you handle puppies, seniors or reactive dogs?

Yes, and private care suits them best. Tell me about your dog at the meet & greet and I'll adapt the walk, pace and route.

What if I need to cancel?

Just let me know as early as you can. I don't charge cancellation fees.

How do I pay?

I invoice weekly for regular walks and up front for overnight stays. I accept Venmo, Zelle, Apple Pay or cash.
